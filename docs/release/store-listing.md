### Full description (Play: 4,000 / App Store: 4,000)

> Garajo keeps your bikes, scooters and cars in one garage — what you spend, what's due, and every document — so nothing catches you by surprise.
>
> **NEVER MISS A DATE**
> • Reminders 30, 7 and 1 day before insurance, PUC and registration expire — at 9 AM, never at night
> • Service reminders when oil, brakes, tyres or battery are due, based on the km you've done
> • A "Coming up" card shows what needs attention next, one tap to renew
>
> **KNOW WHAT YOUR VEHICLE COSTS**
> • Fuel, service and every other expense in one place
> • Monthly and yearly budgets with alerts at 80 % and 100 %
> • Real cost per km and fuel cost per km
> • Month and year charts, category breakdown, CSV export
>
> **SCAN, DON'T TYPE**
> • Photograph both sides of your RC card — Garajo fills in registration number, make, model, dates, engine and chassis numbers
> • Works on your phone; optional Gemini AI for tricky cards
>
> **EVERY VEHICLE, ITS OWN CARE**
> • Bikes, scooters and cars, each with the right service schedule and health score
> • Documents vault for RC, insurance, PUC, licence and warranty
>
> **YOURS, AND PRIVATE**
> • Works offline; backs up to the cloud when you sign in
> • No ads. Delete your account and cloud data anytime from Settings
>
> Available in English, हिन्दी, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português and العربية.
